# 怒り Ikari (Road Rage): shot list and timeline, draft 1

A wordless film of about 72 seconds made in the style of a Japanese colour woodcut (ukiyo-e). A father is bringing a birthday cake home. A truck merges in front of him and gives an apologetic wave, which he takes as a rude one. Both men's tempers rise until they are shouting on a bridge in the rain, and an ambulance gets stuck in the traffic jam they cause behind them.

**Tempo:** 120 BPM in 4/4 at 24 fps. One beat is 12 frames (6 drawings) and one bar is 48 frames (2 s, 24 drawings). Each shot lasts 2, 3 or 4 bars. The film is 36 bars, which is 1728 frames or 72 s.

## Characters (each drawn at one fixed size in every shot)
- **Kenji:** a salaryman in a white shirt and dark tie who drives a small vermilion car. A cake box tied with a ribbon sits on the passenger seat.
- **Hana:** Kenji's small daughter, who waits for him at home.
- **The truck driver:** an older man with a twisted towel headband (hachimaki) and an indigo work jacket. He drives a blue box truck with a wave pattern painted on its side.
- **The ambulance:** a white van with a red stripe and a flashing red light.
- **Anger** is shown as kabuki *kumadori* make-up. Red stripes appear on a face, stroke by stroke, as the man's temper rises. When it peaks, a great woodcut wave rises in the sky behind the men.

## Look (for the style test)
- **The prints:** a black keyblock line with a slight variation in thickness. The colours are flat blocks printed slightly off register, with the wood grain showing in large flat areas. Skies use *bokashi* gradients, with Prussian blue at the top of the sky and at the horizon. The paper is cream washi with visible fibres. Rain is drawn as straight ruled lines, and lightning as a jagged cut.
- **Light:** each time of day is its own set of print colours, so the colour blocks change as the story moves on. Afternoon is pale blue and ochre. Golden hour is persimmon and gold. Sunset is vermilion with an indigo storm above it. Dusk is grey-violet lit by red flashes. Night is indigo, with a gold moon and lantern light.
- **Reused from `ikaros/`:** the camera over larger pages, the hand-placed stop-motion jitter, the cached static pages, the jointed puppets (re-dressed in shirts, jackets and kimono, with ukiyo-e faces), parallel rendering, contact sheets and assembly. **New for this film:** a woodcut print kit to replace the torn-paper kit (keyblock lines, flat blocks with misregistration, wood grain, bokashi), vehicles, kumadori make-up, rain and the wave.

## Sound (Japanese, instrumental, all synthesised)
- **Instruments:** shakuhachi (a breathy bamboo flute), koto (plucked zither), shamisen (plucked, with the *sawari* buzz), taiko drums, kabuki *tsuke* clappers and *hyōshigi* blocks for each *mie*, and a low drone.
- **Arc:**
  - Act I: a calm shakuhachi over koto, in a pentatonic *in* scale.
  - Act II: the shamisen comes in, and the taiko build towards the peak.
  - Act III: the siren cuts through, everything falls silent, and a single koto phrase follows.
  - Act IV: the opening melody returns, gentle.
- **Effects:** traffic, the car horn, braking, rain, thunder, the siren, a door, a laugh made from shakuhachi breath, and the striking of a match for the candle.
